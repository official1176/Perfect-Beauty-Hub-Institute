Beauty tips

Trending tips

Short, practical advice from our studio before your big day.

Bridal

Start bridal skin prep a month early

Keep a simple routine of cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen. Book your facial one to two weeks before the wedding, never the day before.

Hair

Follow keratin aftercare closely

Most smoothening and keratin treatments need the hair kept dry and open for the first few days. Ask your stylist for the exact timing.

Makeup

Pick your HD shade in daylight

Natural light shows the true match for foundation. Bring photos of the looks you like so your artist can plan around your outfit.
